## Order of work and acceptance

1. **Security and release baseline.** Reconcile the migration ledger and current database catalog read-only, obtain and rehearse restore of a backup in an isolated project, and resolve drift using forward-only migrations. Investigate high-severity `picomatch` GHSA-fc5q-39xc-vjpf: the current audit reports no patched version; do not suppress the audit. Re-run the complete security and release gates on the candidate. Verify provider settings separately without exposing credentials.
2. **Newsletter and contact.** The current anonymous newsletter subscription RPC accepts a replacement token hash for an existing address; an attacker may replace that token and then unsubscribe the address. Design mailbox-proof confirmation, safe token rotation, rate limiting, generic responses, consent, unsubscribe, monitoring, and isolated delivery/failure testing before declaring the service safe or operational. Reconcile the copy/status across public routes. Contact, advertising and corrections need persistent monitored intake and privacy controls, not fake success.
3. **News navigation and discovery.** Verify direct and client navigation, back/forward and hash behavior; retain readable scroll targets. Finish accessible category/search controls, selected-state persistence, outside/Escape dismissal, mobile layout, and real-data empty/error states. Breaking flags require expiry and published records; identify the exact obsolete promotional block before removal. This branch adds outside-pointer dismissal and focused tests; it does not certify the whole news redesign.
4. **Article presentation and sharing.** Audit published end-of-article bylines, related content, canonical metadata and actual cover/image behavior with verified media. Test social link previews on supported platforms, CORS/native-share fallback, image attribution and logo fallback. Design the requested additional presentation styles without compromising accessibility or implying unpublished facts.
5. **Protected newsroom.** Design the staged editor and private recovery, preserving author, category, plain text, image rights, accessibility metadata and SEO. Add validated upload/storage, progress, failure cleanup and safe URL handling; arbitrary webpage extraction requires redirect, IP, content-type and size/SSRF defenses. Add accessible fixed modals, feedback, and useful settings/social links and staff password change through authenticated provider flows. Exercise accessibility and role revocation. Resolve the requested hidden-draft versus recovery behavior without making unsaved work public.
6. **Publication and deletion.** Specify and implement public edits as authorized atomic, revision-checked, auditable transitions; never automatically publish private recovery content. Scheduled publication needs timezone, authorization, cancellation, retries and audit. Permanent deletion needs an explicit policy for related versions, uploads, analytics, caches, backups, legal retention and audit; implement capability checks, clear confirmation, race protection and isolated tests before enabling a destructive control. A request to delete does not imply that immutable backups vanish instantly.
7. **Analytics.** Define purpose, consent, retention, staff permissions and disclosure. Implement graphs from genuine aggregated article data, distinguishing daily deduplicated article views from active visitors. Real-time presence and country estimates require an appropriate privacy-preserving collection and reliable provider; never infer them from the existing daily counter or fabricate numbers. Show unavailable on errors, not zero.
8. **Identity and communications.** Review member/staff isolation, owner-admin provisioning, signup, OTP/recovery and password change. Add abuse limits, replay protection and race-safe uniqueness; avoid account enumeration. Verify branded mail, sender authentication and delivery in an isolated environment before operational claims. Ensure staff session and capability checks occur on every protected action.
9. **All eight web areas.** Perform route-by-route audit and complete missing functionality in News, Itan Ilu Mi/history (private rights-cleared media, provenance, consent and corrections), Forum (posts/polls/moderation/rate limits), Map (safe directions and provider-gated API), Business (verified listings, products and protected orders), Tourism (sourced approved records), Radio (verified stream, schedules/archive and reminder limitations), and Who is Who (verified profiles and corrections). Complete member dashboard, saved reading, notifications and offline reading with appropriate privacy and provider gates. Do not invent records or metrics.
10. **Providers, legal and future work.** Verify storage, email, Maps, radio rights/availability, Paystack sandbox/webhooks and payments before claiming or enabling dependent operations. Complete privacy/legal, rights and retention review. Keep native apps deferred and preserve event ticketing, emergency contacts, scholarships/jobs, e-learning, bilingual support and AI assistant as future roadmap items rather than claiming completion.
11. **Release verification.** Test all routes and states at 320/375/768/1024/1440, keyboard/focus, screen reader semantics, reduced motion and reflow. Use isolated synthetic accounts/data to test member/staff boundaries, RLS, stale revisions, audit and provider failure. Run typecheck, production builds, full unit/database and Playwright suites and dependency/security audit on the exact candidate. Record exact evidence; request decisions for blocked scope. Do not merge with missing relevant gates, and do not claim live production behavior from mocks.
